Kalu Waterfall

Malshej Ghat, Maharashtra

A tall monsoon waterfall in the Malshej region, falling off the edge of the plateau.

Kalu Waterfall drops off the edge of the Sahyadri plateau in the Malshej region, and in full monsoon it's one of the taller falls in Maharashtra.

The walk in crosses farmland and streams, and the trail gets muddy fast. On a misty day you'll hear the falls long before you see them.

Before you go

  • Tell someone at home where you're going and when you expect to be back.
  • Monsoon trails are beautiful and slippery. Start early and finish before dark.
  • Water levels change fast after rain. If a crossing looks wrong, turn back.
  • Carry at least two litres of water and something salty to eat.
  • Wear shoes with real grip; wet rock and laterite are slippery.
  • Pack out everything you carry in. Leave no trace.
