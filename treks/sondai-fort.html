Sondai Fort

Near Karjat, Maharashtra

A small fort near Karjat with a short climb, a ladder near the top and rock-cut water cisterns.

Sondai is a small fort near Karjat: a short, friendly climb that makes a good first trek. Near the top a ladder helps you up the last rock step.

On the summit you'll find old rock-cut water cisterns and views across to Matheran and the Morbe dam.

Before you go

  • Tell someone at home where you're going and when you expect to be back.
  • Monsoon trails are beautiful and slippery. Start early and finish before dark.
  • Carry at least two litres of water and something salty to eat.
  • Wear shoes with real grip; wet rock and laterite are slippery.
  • Pack out everything you carry in. Leave no trace.
