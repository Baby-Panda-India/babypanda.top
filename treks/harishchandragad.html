Harishchandragad

Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra

The great Sahyadri fort: Konkan Kada's sheer cliff, an ancient cave temple and Taramati peak.

Harishchandragad is one of the best-loved treks in the Sahyadris. On top you'll find the Harishchandreshwar temple, the Kedareshwar cave with its Shiva linga standing in water, and Konkan Kada, a vast concave cliff that drops away toward the Konkan.

The climb is long and the plateau is big. Give yourself time on top, and if the clouds lift at Konkan Kada, stay and watch.

Before you go

  • Tell someone at home where you're going and when you expect to be back.
  • Monsoon trails are beautiful and slippery. Start early and finish before dark.
  • Carry at least two litres of water and something salty to eat.
  • Wear shoes with real grip; wet rock and laterite are slippery.
  • Pack out everything you carry in. Leave no trace.
