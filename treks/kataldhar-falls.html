Kataldhar Waterfall

Near Lonavla, Maharashtra

A monsoon waterfall in a rock bowl, reached on foot through forest below Lonavla.

Kataldhar is a monsoon waterfall that pours into a rock bowl in the forest below Lonavla. There's no road to it; you reach it on foot, down through the trees and over wet rock.

The descent is steep and slippery in the rain, and it's an even steeper climb back out. It's worth it once you're standing under the falls.

Before you go

  • Tell someone at home where you're going and when you expect to be back.
  • Monsoon trails are beautiful and slippery. Start early and finish before dark.
  • Water levels change fast after rain. If a crossing looks wrong, turn back.
  • Carry at least two litres of water and something salty to eat.
  • Wear shoes with real grip; wet rock and laterite are slippery.
  • Pack out everything you carry in. Leave no trace.
