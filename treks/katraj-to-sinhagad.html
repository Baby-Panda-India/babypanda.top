K2S: Katraj to Sinhagad

Pune, Maharashtra

Pune's classic long ridge walk, hill after hill from Katraj Ghat to the walls of Sinhagad.

K2S is the long ridge walk from Katraj Ghat to Sinhagad along the hills south of Pune. It's less a single climb than a string of them: up one hill, down, and up the next, again and again.

It's a test of stamina more than technique. Pace yourself, carry more water than you think you need, and save something for the final climb to Sinhagad.

Before you go

  • Tell someone at home where you're going and when you expect to be back.
  • Monsoon trails are beautiful and slippery. Start early and finish before dark.
  • Carry at least two litres of water and something salty to eat.
  • Wear shoes with real grip; wet rock and laterite are slippery.
  • Pack out everything you carry in. Leave no trace.
