Tahuli Peak

Near Kalyan, Maharashtra

A quiet peak near Kalyan with rock pinnacles on top and long views over the plains.

Tahuli sits in the hills near Kalyan and Ambernath, a short trip from Mumbai but far quieter than the famous forts. Rock pinnacles stand on its summit plateau.

The trail climbs through grass and scrub with few shady stretches, so start early and carry plenty of water.

Before you go

  • Tell someone at home where you're going and when you expect to be back.
  • Monsoon trails are beautiful and slippery. Start early and finish before dark.
  • Carry at least two litres of water and something salty to eat.
  • Wear shoes with real grip; wet rock and laterite are slippery.
  • Pack out everything you carry in. Leave no trace.
