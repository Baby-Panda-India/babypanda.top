Moola Kona Waterfall

Tirupati district, Andhra Pradesh

A day of Andhra forest falls, from a hidden rock pool to Kailasakona beside its temple.

BPT 14 took us to the Eastern Ghats near the Andhra–Tamil Nadu border for a day of waterfalls: Moola Kona, the small rock pool at Thotti Kona, and Kailasakona beside its temple.

Thotti Kona is a hidden fall and natural rock pool just before Kailasakona, about 110 to 120 km from Chennai. “Thotti” means tub or basin, and that's what it is: a compact pool with water dropping into it from a short height. It's quieter than the temple falls nearby.

Kailasakona, or Kaulesha Kona, is about 40 feet high, in Narayanavanam mandal, with a temple to Shiva and Parvati beside it. From the car park on the Uttukottai–Puttur–Tirupati road (NH 40), a stepped path, lit at night, reaches the main falls in three to five minutes. On the way in there are two smaller falls, 4 to 6 feet high.

The Moola Kona reel has a song in it. Watch to the end.

Before you go

  • Tell someone at home where you're going and when you expect to be back.
  • Water levels change fast after rain. If a crossing looks wrong, turn back.
  • Carry at least two litres of water and something salty to eat.
  • Wear shoes with real grip; wet rock and laterite are slippery.
  • Pack out everything you carry in. Leave no trace.
