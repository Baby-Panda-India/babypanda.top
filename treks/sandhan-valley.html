Sandhan Valley

Bhandardara, Maharashtra

The Valley of Shadows: a narrow canyon cut by water, deep enough to block out the sun.

Sandhan Valley, near Samrad village in the Bhandardara region, is a narrow canyon carved by water through the Sahyadri basalt. The walls are close and tall enough that much of it stays in shade all day, hence the name Valley of Shadows.

The route squeezes between the walls over boulders and through pools, some of them waist-deep. It's a trek to do after the monsoon, once the water has dropped; during heavy rain the canyon is dangerous.

Before you go

  • Tell someone at home where you're going and when you expect to be back.
  • Monsoon trails are beautiful and slippery. Start early and finish before dark.
  • Water levels change fast after rain. If a crossing looks wrong, turn back.
  • Carry at least two litres of water and something salty to eat.
  • Wear shoes with real grip; wet rock and laterite are slippery.
  • Pack out everything you carry in. Leave no trace.
